Two sisters, one secret sponge recipe

Bake Away started on a kitchen table, with a hand-me-down mixer and our grandmother's butter cake written on the back of an envelope. We still fold every batter by hand, cream real butter with jaggery-brown sugar, and let each sponge rest overnight before it meets its frosting.

No premixes and no shortcuts. Just the slow, happy kind of baking that makes a kitchen smell like a birthday.
