Privacy notice · United Kingdom GDPR

Privacy policy

This notice explains how Trace Solutions Global (“we”) uses personal information collected on tracesolutionsglobal.com. We are the controller. You can reach us at sales@tracesolutionsglobal.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, business name, phone, email, business type and message.
  • Review form. Your name, optional business and country, star rating and review. Reviews appear on the homepage with your name, business and country, and we may hide or remove any review.
  • Concept Builder. Your name, phone, email, location, business details, chosen pages, features, style and the answers you give. That brief is saved as a lead on our private dashboard.

We use this information to reply to you, prepare a proposal, publish your review, and run the project. The legal basis is steps you ask us to take before a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and showing genuine client feedback.

Who processes it

  • Web3Forms emails the contact form, review alerts and concept briefs to sales@tracesolutionsglobal.com.
  • Supabase stores reviews and project leads. The database is in the region we chose when the project was created.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a form was sent by a person, when a site key is configured.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. It does not store the form contents.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and concept briefs are kept while we are talking to you and for up to 24 months after the last contact, unless a longer period is needed for a contract or a legal claim. Approved reviews stay on the site until you ask us to remove them, or we take them down. Hidden or rejected reviews are deleted or kept only as long as we need to deal with spam.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, corrections, deletion, or a restriction on how we use it. Email sales@tracesolutionsglobal.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
