Terms of service

These terms apply when Trace Solutions Global (“we”, “us”) designs, builds or looks after a website or marketing service for you (“you”, the client). Your proposal sets out the details of your project: scope, price, payment schedule and timeline. If your proposal and these terms ever disagree, the proposal wins.

We are based in Sri Lanka and currently work with clients in the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia and Sri Lanka.

1. Concepts and proposals

  • Concepts made in our Concept Builder are free and don't commit you to anything. Package and timeline estimates shown there are a guide, not a quote.
  • After we review your concept we send a written proposal with a fixed price in US dollars. A proposal is valid for 30 days.
  • Every website fits one of three packages, depending on its size: Starter (usually 1–4 weeks), Growth (usually 3–5 weeks) or Pro (usually 5–8 weeks). Your proposal confirms the package, the price and the timeline for your project.
  • The project starts once you accept the proposal in writing (email or WhatsApp is fine) and pay any deposit it asks for.

2. Prices and payment

  • The fixed price covers the pages, features and revision rounds listed in your proposal. Work outside that scope is quoted separately before we start it.
  • Payments are due on the dates or milestones in your proposal. Invoices are payable within 7 days unless your proposal says otherwise.
  • Third-party costs such as domain names, hosting, paid plugins, stock photos, advertising budgets and payment-gateway fees are paid by you, either directly or passed through at cost, as set out in your proposal.
  • If a payment is more than 14 days late we may pause work until it is paid.

3. Your content and approvals

  • You supply your logo, text, photos, prices and other business information, and confirm you have the right to use them. We aren't responsible for checking that content you give us is accurate or licensed.
  • Timelines assume we receive content and feedback when agreed. Delays in content or approvals move the launch date.
  • If a project is on hold waiting for you for more than 60 days, we may close it and invoice for the work done so far.

4. Revisions

Your proposal lists the number of revision rounds included. A revision round is one set of combined changes to work we've delivered. Extra rounds, or changes that alter the agreed scope, are quoted before we do them.

5. Ownership

  • Once you have paid in full, you own the final website design and the content made specifically for you, and we hand over access to your domain, hosting and site accounts.
  • We keep ownership of our own tools, templates and code libraries, and give you a permanent licence to use them as part of your website.
  • Unless you tell us otherwise in writing, we may show the finished website in our portfolio and on social media.

6. Launch, support and hosting

  • We fix bugs in the work we delivered free of charge for 30 days after launch. A bug is something that doesn't work as agreed; new features and content changes are not bugs.
  • Ongoing updates, hosting management, social media and ads are separate services with their own monthly price, if you choose them.
  • We aren't responsible for problems caused by changes made by you or a third party, or by outages at hosting, domain or other third-party providers.

7. Cancellation

Either of us can end a project by giving written notice. You pay for work done up to that point, and any deposit covers that work first. We then hand over the work you have paid for.

We take care to build websites that work well, but we can't guarantee search rankings, sales or uninterrupted availability. Our total liability for a project is limited to the amount you paid us for it. Nothing in these terms limits liability that can't be limited by law.
