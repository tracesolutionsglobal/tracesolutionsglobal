Place names and UK postcodes from postcodes.io (Ordnance Survey Open Names). Other countries use OpenStreetMap place search. Businesses, public phone numbers, emails, websites, WhatsApp, social links and opening hours come from OpenStreetMap. Deep crawl also checks ways and area relations, and the place index adds nearby named places. A business with nothing published shows “Not listed”. Entire city / area uses the OpenStreetMap boundary, which is too large for some cities on the free service. All mapped businesses needs a radius of 15 miles or less.
